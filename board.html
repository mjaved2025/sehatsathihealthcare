Board Members

Meet the people helping shape the vision and direction of SehatSathi.

Nawab Shah Al Hanif

Director

Driven by a strong commitment to improving healthcare in rural communities, Nawab Shah Al Hanif aims to bridge the gap between rural and urban healthcare facilities.

With a focus on health awareness, preventive care, and early detection, his vision is to empower rural communities to prevent major diseases and adopt healthier lifestyles.

The goal is to bring quality healthcare, modern technology, and the benefits of advanced urban healthcare facilities closer to rural people, ensuring that everyone has access to the knowledge, resources, and care needed to live a healthier and better life.

Md Sabenoor

Director

The vision is to create a healthier rural India where people have access to the same healthcare awareness, technology, and quality services available in cities.

Md Sabenoor believes that prevention begins with awareness and that every individual deserves the opportunity to understand their health, prevent major diseases, and live a healthy and fulfilling life.

Through technology-driven healthcare, education, and community-focused initiatives, the goal is to connect rural communities with modern healthcare and make quality healthcare accessible to all.
