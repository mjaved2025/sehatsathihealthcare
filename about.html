Who we are

Technology with a community-first approach.

SehatSathi Healthcare is being built around a simple idea: healthcare should be easier to discover, coordinate and access.

Our approach combines local partnerships with digital tools so that people can find relevant services without unnecessary complexity.

Business activity

What we do

SehatSathi Healthcare Private Limited operates as a healthcare access, health screening, health guidance, and patient-support company.

The company’s objective is to make affordable and convenient healthcare services more accessible to customers by providing basic health screening, creating digital health records, facilitating consultations with registered doctors, assisting with specialist doctor appointments and referrals, supporting customers with follow-ups, and developing a trusted network of registered doctors, diagnostic centres, clinics, hospitals, and other authorized healthcare service providers.
